Job Form Autofill: privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Job Form Autofill is a Chrome extension that fills job application forms with answers you saved earlier. In short: your data stays on your computer. Nothing is collected, sent, sold or shared.

What it stores

The answers you give on job application forms (for example your name, email, phone number, links, work history and answers to application questions), the questions it hasn't seen an answer for yet, and the website name where each new question was seen.

Where it is stored

Only in Chrome's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your own computer, in your browser profile. It is not synced to your Google account and not sent to any server. The developer never receives it.

What it never stores

Passwords, government ID numbers (such as SSN, Aadhaar, PAN or passport numbers), bank or card details, and one-time codes. Fields that ask for these are skipped.

Network use

The extension makes no network requests of its own. It has no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no remote code. It uses no AI services.

Website access

It reads and fills a page only when you press Fill this page, Save answers from this page or the keyboard shortcut, or on sites you turned on yourself from the popup. You can turn sites off any time on the Manage answers page.

Your control

You can see, edit and delete every saved answer on the Manage answers page, download them as a backup file, or delete everything at once. Removing the extension deletes all its data.

Contact

Questions or problems: open an issue at github.com/R-Dilipkumar/job-form-autofill/issues.
